# OTH Map — how to use it

**Open it** from the link Stef sends you, on a laptop.

**The team code.** The first time on each browser it asks for the team code. Ask Stef for it. You type it once; after that it goes straight to sign-in.

**Sign in** by picking your name. Not on the list? Anyone already signed in can add you: *your avatar (top right) → Team → Add teammate*.

**Your floor.** *Floors* shows all eight, who owns each, and how far each has got. *Continue on L1 →* opens your floor on the tab you used last. Each floor has three tabs:

- **Survey** — tag what you see in each frame of the walkthrough. **← →** move between frames, **N** jumps to the next untagged one, **T** types a name, **Enter** tags (the frame stays in view until you move on), **Del** bins a useless frame, **G** shows them all as a grid. Drag on the frame to box the thing you mean.
- **Review** — the proposals the labelling agent made, one at a time: **A** accept, **R** reject, **S** skip, **L** the list.
- **Build** — the map itself, in six steps, in this order:
  1. **Floorplan** — upload the floor's plan, set its scale with *Sheet scale* (OTH's plans are A3 at 1:1500), and line it up with the floor below.
  2. **Areas & rooms** — draw the open areas (**A**) and the rooms and shops (**R**).
  3. **Pathways** — draw where people walk (**P**).
  4. **Entrances & lifts** — mark each entrance (**E**) and each lift or stair stop (**L**).
  5. **Bind places** — click a room, then pick the place that is in it.
  6. **Dressing** — details and 3D models, last.

**The 3D preview** in Build shows only when you press **3D**, bottom left of the plan. Your browser remembers whether it is on.

**Mistakes** are fine. Every delete and every decision shows **Undo** for a few seconds, and nothing asks "are you sure?".

**Publish** (top right) is for the whole building. It works only once nothing blocks it, and its list says what is left, floor by floor.

**A yellow "isn't filed under a floor" bar** in Survey means footage with no floor. Press *File under …* only if it was filmed on the floor you are on.
