# OTH services extracted from `images/othhubguide2020.pdf`

> Source: One Tampines Hub Guide (2020 edition). PDF was text-extractable (36 pages,
> via pdfplumber). The guide describes facilities by **floor and named zone**, NOT by
> unit numbers (e.g. `#01-21`). Every entry below uses "not listed" for unit numbers
> because the PDF never assigns one. Retail tenants inside Festive Mall are not
> enumerated in this PDF — only Hub-level facilities and operators are. Page numbers
> reference the PDF's physical page order (cover = page 1).

# Notes on the PDF

- The PDF assigns facilities to **floor + named zone** rather than HDB-style unit
  numbers (`#01-21` etc). PA's separate "OTH Tenants Directory List" likely has
  unit numbers for individual tenants — covered in Task 6.
- Pages 32 and 33 contain floor-plan diagrams (referenced by label-only text).
  Garbled doubled glyphs appear (e.g. "GymnaGsyiummn a -s iLu7m - L7") because
  pdfplumber duplicated overlapping label layers — text is still interpretable.
- L1 floor-plan (page 32) labels the following items on L1: Public Service Centre,
  Arena @ OTH, Town Square, Bicycle Bays, South Arrival Plaza. Festive Walk and
  Festive Plaza are obvious but not separately labelled on the floor-plan.
- L2 floor-plan (page 32) labels: Team Sports Hall, Festive Arts Theatre, Tampines
  Regional Library, Swimming Pools (L6 above), Gym (L7 above).
- The PDF does NOT list individual Festive Mall tenants (FairPrice, etc.) — those
  must come from Task 6 web sources.
- No mention of a polyclinic in the PDF (only Family Medicine Clinic on L3,
  Community Health Centre on L3, Senior Care Centre on L4). The Tampines
  Polyclinic is a separate building — see gaps notes.
