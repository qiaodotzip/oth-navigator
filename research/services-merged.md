# Merged OTH service directory (L1 + L2)

> Cross-references the OTH Hub Guide PDF (`images/othhubguide2020.pdf`) with the PA
> Festive Mall Directory at https://www.pa.gov.sg/our-network/our-tampines-hub/festive-mall-directory/
> (the directory page itself states "Accurate as of 29 April 2026"), plus Wikipedia
> for the library. Where unit numbers exist in the PA directory, they take priority
> over the PDF (which doesn't list unit numbers). Hours come from the PDF page 31
> "Key Facilities Operating Hours" panel. Accessibility details that are not
> explicitly published anywhere accessible are marked "unverified".

## How to read

`Provider` = managing agency or operator. `Source(s)` always cites at least one URL
or PDF reference. Accessibility notes only assert what is verifiable; everything
else is logged in `gaps.md`.

## L1 - Public Service Centre (PSC)

- **Floor / Unit:** L1, #01-21 (counter occupied by ServiceSG Centre + e2i; PSC operates as the umbrella that hosts these and other agency counters)
- **Provider:** People's Association (lead agency); houses 13 government agency counters
- **Accessibility notes:** unverified - no published accessibility statement, but the hub is BCA Green Mark certified and is a flagship integrated facility
- **Source(s):**
  - PDF page 9 (description), page 31 (hours)
  - https://www.pa.gov.sg/our-network/our-tampines-hub/festive-mall-directory/ (lists ServiceSG Centre at #01-21)
- **Hours:** Mon-Sun 9am-10pm; closed on Public Holidays (PDF p.31)
- **Last verified:** 2026-05-22
